About Allyson

Meet Allyson

  • M.A. Educational Studies
  • MBA
  • B.A. Communications
  • PVPUSD Parent & Alumna

For Allyson Lehrer, serving on the Palos Verdes Peninsula Unified School District (PVPUSD) School Board is more than an opportunity to give back. It is the chance to serve the community that helped shape her life and now shapes the lives of her own children.

Allyson's family moved to Palos Verdes when she was four years old, and she attended PVPUSD schools from kindergarten through graduation, attending Vista Grande Elementary, Ridgecrest Intermediate School, and Palos Verdes High School.

As a student, she embraced the many opportunities that make PVPUSD exceptional. She was a student-athlete, served in Associated Student Body leadership, and benefited from an Individualized Education Plan (IEP) that helped her reach her full potential. Those experiences taught her the importance of outstanding educators, supportive schools, and believing in every student's ability to succeed. They inspired the guiding principle of her campaign: #APlaceForEveryChild.

After earning a Bachelor of Arts in Communications with a minor in Jewish Studies, Allyson built a career dedicated to education and organizational leadership. She later earned both a Master of Arts in Educational Studies and a Master of Business Administration (MBA), combining expertise in education, leadership, and finance.

As Allyson and her husband, Dr. Ian Lehrer, built their life together, they lived in California, Texas, and New York, where Allyson gained firsthand experience working in education and collaborating with schools, educators, nonprofit organizations, and community partners. These experiences broadened her perspective and reinforced a simple truth: outstanding schools are built through exceptional educators, engaged families, responsible leadership, and strong community partnerships.

When it came time to raise a family, Allyson and Ian knew exactly where they wanted to call home. They returned to Palos Verdes so their children could experience the same outstanding schools and close-knit community that had such a lasting impact on Allyson's own life.

Today, Allyson and Ian are raising their two young children in the community they proudly call home. Their oldest daughter attends Lunada Bay Elementary, and their youngest will soon follow, giving Allyson the unique perspective of a former student and current parent.

Allyson currently serves as Vice Chair of the PVPUSD Education Advisory Committee, where she works alongside district leadership, educators, parents, and fellow committee members to strengthen educational opportunities and support student success. Her professional background in strategic planning and partnership development has equipped her to make thoughtful, data-informed decisions while bringing people together around shared goals.

Allyson believes the best decisions are made through collaboration, transparency, and an unwavering commitment to students. She is running for School Board to preserve PVPUSD's tradition of excellence while preparing students for the future, supporting educators, practicing responsible fiscal stewardship, and ensuring every child has the opportunity to thrive.
