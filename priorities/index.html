How I'll Advocate for You

My Priorities

As a proud PVPUSD graduate, parent of one current student, another soon to enroll, and Vice Chair of the Education Advisory Committee, I believe our district has an incredible foundation. My goal is not to reinvent what makes PVPUSD exceptional, but to build on our strengths, address emerging challenges, and ensure #APlaceForEveryChild.

Preparing Students for the Future

Today's students will graduate into a world shaped by rapid innovation, artificial intelligence, and careers that continue to evolve. Our responsibility is not simply to prepare students for their next test, but for their next chapter in life.

I believe students need both technical proficiency and the uniquely human skills that will always matter: critical thinking, communication, creativity, collaboration, curiosity, adaptability, and empathy. These skills prepare students not only to navigate change but to lead it.

PVPUSD has long been recognized for academic excellence. By continuing to invest in innovative learning opportunities while strengthening these essential lifelong skills, we can ensure our graduates are prepared for college, careers, civic engagement, and a future we cannot yet fully predict.

Safe & Supportive Schools

Students learn best when they feel safe, valued, and connected.

Creating safe schools means more than maintaining secure campuses. It means fostering learning environments where every student feels physically safe, emotionally supported, respected, and empowered to reach their full potential.

I believe in partnering with families, educators, students, and the broader community to promote positive student mental health and well-being, strengthen positive school culture, and ensure every child has access to the support they need to succeed. Safe and supportive schools are the foundation upon which academic excellence is built.

Fiscal Stewardship

Strong schools begin with responsible financial leadership. Every budget decision should reflect our values by investing wisely in both the people and programs that make PVPUSD exceptional while planning for the long-term sustainability of our district.

Fiscal stewardship means looking beyond annual budgets and making thoughtful decisions that preserve educational excellence for the students of today, and future generations.It also means seeking creative solutions to maximize our resources. Whether through grants, corporate partnerships, philanthropy, or operational efficiencies, we should pursue opportunities that enhance educational experiences without compromising fiscal responsibility.

Our teachers and staff are our greatest asset. While district budgets have limitations, we should continually explore innovative ways to support and retain outstanding educators by strengthening community partnerships and identifying additional resources that complement traditional compensation.

Responsible stewardship is about ensuring every dollar advances student success while protecting the excellence that families have come to expect from PVPUSD.

Enrollment

California's public schools are funded largely through an Average Daily Attendance (ADA) model. This means districts receive state funding based on the average number of students who attend school each day, not simply the number who are enrolled. While attendance determines funding, enrollment and attendance are closely connected. The more families who choose PVPUSD, the greater our opportunity to engage students, strengthen attendance, and invest in exceptional educational experiences.

As families have more educational choices than ever before, PVPUSD must continue to be a district they proudly choose, remain committed to, and enthusiastically recommend to others.

Growing enrollment is not simply about increasing numbers. It is about continually earning the trust of families by providing exceptional academics, outstanding teachers, safe and supportive schools, and opportunities for every student to succeed.

My campaign is built around one simple belief: #APlaceForEveryChild.

When every child feels welcomed, challenged, supported, and inspired, families choose to stay. When families have confidence in their schools, they become our greatest ambassadors. By investing in educational excellence, fostering a culture of belonging, and strengthening the partnership between schools and families, we can build a district that thrives for generations.
