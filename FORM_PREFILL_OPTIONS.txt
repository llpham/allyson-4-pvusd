PRE-FILLED FORM LINKS — an option for the campaign website
=============================================================================

Prepared for: Allyson Lehrer campaign
Re:           Sign-up form on the website ("Let's Connect" section)
Date:         July 2026

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
SHORT VERSION
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------

The website can open your Google Form with certain answers ALREADY FILLED IN,
depending on which button the visitor clicked.

For example: someone who clicks "Request a Yard Sign" could land on the form
with the yard-sign option already checked, instead of having to find and check
it themselves.

This is optional. The site works fine as-is. We're asking whether you want it,
and if so, which buttons should pre-fill what.

Nothing needs to change on your end unless you say yes.


-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THIS LOOKS LIKE TO A VISITOR
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------

Today, every button leads to the same place: the sign-up form, blank.

  "Join the Movement"     -->  sign-up form (blank)
  "Request a Yard Sign"   -->  sign-up form (blank)

With pre-filled links, buttons can lead to the same form in different states:

  "Join the Movement"     -->  sign-up form (blank)
  "Request a Yard Sign"   -->  sign-up form, "Display a yard sign" pre-checked
  "Volunteer"             -->  sign-up form, "Volunteer" pre-checked

It is the SAME form and the SAME response spreadsheet either way. Only the
starting state differs. Fewer clicks for the visitor, which usually means more
people finish the form.


-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS — please read before deciding
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. THE VISITOR CAN SEE AND CHANGE ANYTHING WE PRE-FILL.
   A pre-filled answer is just a normal answer that starts out filled in. The
   visitor can uncheck it, type over it, or ignore it. It is not locked and it
   is not hidden.

   Because of this, pre-filling is only appropriate for convenience — checking
   a box the person clearly already wants checked. It should never be used for
   anything the campaign needs to be certain about.

2. IT IS NOT A RELIABLE WAY TO TRACK WHERE SOMEONE CAME FROM.
   You might be tempted to pre-fill a hidden "source" field to see which button
   people used. It will be roughly right, but anyone can edit it, and the field
   is visible on the form, which looks odd to visitors. If you want real
   traffic data, that's a separate conversation (site analytics).

3. THE LINKS BREAK IF A QUESTION IS DELETED AND RE-ADDED.
   Each question on your form has a hidden ID number. Pre-filled links point at
   those IDs.

   - Editing a question's WORDING is fine. The link keeps working.
   - DELETING a question and creating a new one gives it a new ID. The old link
     stops pre-filling.

   The important part: when this breaks, THERE IS NO ERROR MESSAGE. The form
   simply opens blank, and nobody notices until you wonder why yard-sign
   requests dropped off. So: if the form gets significantly rebuilt, tell your
   website contact so the links can be refreshed.

4. SOME QUESTION TYPES CAN'T BE PRE-FILLED.
   File uploads cannot be pre-filled at all. Dates and times can, but are
   fussy. Text boxes, multiple choice, checkboxes, and dropdowns all work fine.


-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
IF YOU WANT THIS — HOW TO GET US WHAT WE NEED
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------

You (whoever owns the Google account the form lives in) generate a special
link, and send it to your website contact. It takes about two minutes.

  1. Open your form at https://forms.google.com and click into it.

  2. Click the three-dot menu ( : ) in the top-right corner of the page.

  3. Choose "Get pre-filled link".

  4. The form opens in a preview mode. Fill in ONLY the answers you want to
     start out pre-filled. Leave everything else blank.

     Example: to make a yard-sign button, check ONLY the yard-sign option.
     Don't type a name or email — those should stay blank for the visitor.

  5. Scroll to the bottom and click "Get link".

  6. A bar appears at the bottom of the screen. Click "COPY LINK".

  7. Paste that link into an email, and label what it's for.

Repeat steps 2-7 for each different pre-filled version you want.

WHAT TO SEND BACK — one line per button, like this:

    Yard sign button  -->  https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSex.../viewform?usp=pp_url&entry.1234567890=Display+a+yard+sign

    Volunteer button  -->  https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSex.../viewform?usp=pp_url&entry.1234567890=Volunteer

The links look long and messy. That's normal — please paste them exactly as
copied, without editing or shortening them.

QUESTIONS FOR YOU TO DECIDE
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------

A. Do you want pre-filled links at all? (Perfectly fine to say no.)

B. If yes, which buttons should pre-fill something, and what?
   The buttons currently on the site are:

     - "Join the Movement"       (top of page, gold button)
     - "Request a Yard Sign"     (top of page, outlined button)
     - "Join the Movement"       (in the "Let's Connect" section)

   The most likely candidate is "Request a Yard Sign".

C. Should "Request a Yard Sign" at the top of the page jump STRAIGHT to the
   form, or keep scrolling down to the "Let's Connect" section first?

   - Straight to the form: fewer steps, likely more sign-ups, but the visitor
     skips past the section text.
   - Scroll to the section first: the visitor reads the context around the
     form before starting it.

   There's no wrong answer — it's a question of whether you'd rather people
   read the message or complete the form.
