Endorsements & Gratitude

Thank you for supporting my campaign.

Whether you have endorsed this campaign as a trusted community leader or supported me as a neighbor, parent, or community member, I am deeply grateful for your confidence and your shared belief in #APlaceForEveryChild.

Every endorsement, donation, and word of encouragement reminds me that this campaign is powered by people who care deeply about the future of PVPUSD. Your support makes it possible to connect with voters, share our vision, and build a campaign rooted in thoughtful leadership, collaboration, and a commitment to every student.

I am truly honored to have your trust and support. Thank you for being part of this campaign and this community.

“I’m proud to endorse Allyson Lehrer for PVPUSD School Board. Allyson brings an even-keeled, level-headed approach to leadership, along with the heart and experience of an educator. She has real skin in the game as a longtime member of this community and PVPUSD graduate who returned to raise her family here. I know she will approach every issue thoughtfully, pragmatically, and with students, families, educators, and our community at the center of her decisions.

I’m confident Allyson will be a steady, thoughtful voice on the School Board, and I’m honored to support her.”

Paul SeoMayor, City of Rancho Palos Verdes

“As a proud graduate of the PVPUSD schools and the mother of two young children, Allyson understands the importance of great schools. Her background in both education and business, combined with her dedication to the role, make her an ideal candidate for the School Board.”

Linda KurtCurrent Member, Board of Education; Retired PVPUSD Teacher

“I’ve known Allyson for nearly 10 years, since we first worked together at a school in Texas, and I know she would be an excellent addition to the School Board. She is a strategic, results-oriented leader with strong acumen for driving enrollment, managing budgets, and collaborating across a school community to achieve shared goals. Allyson brings thoughtful leadership, sound judgment, and a big heart to everything she does, and I know she would bring those qualities to serving PVPUSD students, families, and teachers.”

Stephen SchwartzFounder, Pathfinders Academic Consulting

“I am pleased to endorse Allyson Lehrer for the PVPUSD Board. There is every reason to vote for her. She is a product of our school system. She has an extensive educational and professional background in education. And most important, she has a wonderful family of young children either in the system or about to be in the system. She has, as is said, “Skin in the game.” Further, she is a calm and open minded person committed to letting all voices be heard... she is an advocate for all.”

George LewisCoucilmember, City of Rancho Palos Verdes

Al MuratschiCalifornia State Assemblymember
