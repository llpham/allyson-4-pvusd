About Allyson

Meet Allyson Lehrer

  • Local Mom
  • Experienced Education Leader
  • Community Volunteer
  • Palos Verdes Native
  • M.A. Educational Studies
  • MBA
  • B.A. Communications
  • PVPUSD Parent & Alumna

As a proud PVPUSD graduate, parent of two young students, and Vice Chair of the PVPUSD Education Advisory Committee, I am deeply invested in the future of our schools. My experiences as a student, education professional, and community leader have given me a unique understanding of what it takes to help every child succeed.

I’m running for School Board to preserve PVPUSD’s tradition of excellence while preparing our students for the future through collaborative leadership, fiscal responsibility, and a commitment to ensuring #APlaceForEveryChild.

How I'll Advocate for You

My Priorities

Preparing Students for the Future

Preparing students for success in a rapidly changing world by fostering both technical proficiency and the uniquely human skills of critical thinking, communication, creativity, collaboration, curiosity, adaptability, and empathy.

Safe & Supportive Schools

Creating learning environments that prioritize student mental health and well-being, where every student feels physically and emotionally safe, supported, and ready to learn.

Fiscal Stewardship

Making thoughtful financial decisions that protect both our people and our programs while strengthening our district for the future.

Enrollment

Building a district where families proudly enroll, stay, and recommend PVPUSD schools, because we ensure #APlaceForEveryChild.

Community Conversations

Let's Connect

Meeting members of the community, active listening, and maintaining an open dialogue are extremely important to me. Throughout my campaign, and continuing as a Governing Board Member, I am committed to staying connected with students, parents, educators, and community members.

Whether we're meeting over coffee, taking a neighborhood walk, or gathering with a small group, I would love the opportunity to hear your ideas, questions, and perspectives about our schools. Every voice matters, and I value conversations with members of our community who bring different experiences, viewpoints, and priorities to the table.

Schedule an Individual or Group Conversation with me:

  • Coffee Chat
  • Walk & Talk
  • Run Together
  • Neighborhood Gathering
  • Community Meeting

Every conversation helps me better understand what matters most to our community and ensures our schools remain focused on what matters most: #APlaceforEveryChild.
