About Allyson

Meet Allyson Lehrer

  • M.A. Educational Studies
  • MBA
  • B.A. Communications
  • PVPUSD Parent & Alumna

Allyson Lehrer is a proud graduate of Palos Verdes Peninsula Unified School District (PVPUSD), a dedicated parent, educational leader, and community volunteer. She is running for the PVPUSD School Board to help preserve the district's tradition of excellence while ensuring every student has the opportunity to thrive.

Allyson's family moved to Palos Verdes when she was four years old, and she attended PVPUSD schools from kindergarten through twelfth grade. During her time as a student, she embraced the many opportunities that define the PVPUSD experience. She competed as a student athlete, served in Associated Student Body leadership, and benefited from the support of educators who helped her reach her full potential through an Individualized Education Plan (IEP). These experiences shaped her belief that every child deserves to be seen, supported, challenged, and empowered to succeed.

After college, Allyson lived in Los Angeles, where she met her husband, Dr. Ian Lehrer. Together, they embarked on an adventure that took them from Los Angeles to Fort Worth, Texas for his medical internship, and then to New York for his residency training. When they moved to Texas, Allyson began her career in education by working in a school environment, giving her first professional insight into the important role educators, administrators, and communities play in supporting student success. Her continued work in education, including experiences in both Texas and New York, broadened her perspective on different school systems and approaches to serving students and families. These experiences reinforced her belief that strong schools are built through dedicated educators, engaged communities, and thoughtful leadership.

When Allyson & Ian decided to start a family, they knew they wanted to return to California to be closer to family and raise their children in a community that shared their values. As their oldest daughter approached school age, they made the intentional decision to return to Palos Verdes so their children could experience the same outstanding schools and close-knit community that had such a meaningful impact on Allyson's own life.

Today, Allyson and Ian are raising their two young children in the community that shaped her. Their oldest daughter attends Lunada Bay, and their youngest will soon follow, giving Allyson the unique perspective of both a former student and a current parent.

Professionally, Allyson has built a career leading strategic initiatives, developing partnerships, and helping mission-driven organizations expand their impact. She earned a Bachelor of Arts in Communications, a Master of Business Administration (MBA), and a Master of Arts in Educational Studies, providing her with a unique blend of expertise in leadership, organizational management, and educational practice.

Throughout her career, Allyson has worked alongside schools, educators, nonprofit organizations, and community partners to strengthen educational opportunities and improve outcomes for students and families. She currently serves as Vice Chair of the PVPUSD Education Advisory Committee, where she collaborates with district leadership, educators, parents, and fellow committee members to support student success and strengthen community engagement.

Allyson believes the best decisions are made through collaboration, open communication, and a steadfast focus on what is best for students. She is committed to listening to families, supporting educators, maintaining fiscal responsibility, and ensuring PVPUSD continues to provide an exceptional educational experience with a place for every child.

What I'll fight for

Priorities

Fiscal Stewardship

Supporting outstanding educators, investing in exceptional programs, and ensuring the long-term financial health of PVPUSD.

A strong school district requires thoughtful financial stewardship and accountability. As a public institution entrusted with taxpayer dollars, PVPUSD must prioritize transparency, responsible budgeting, and open communication with our community.

I believe fiscal responsibility is about making thoughtful, long-term decisions that strengthen our district as a whole. That means supporting and retaining outstanding teachers and staff, investing in the programs that enrich students' educational experiences, and ensuring every dollar is used wisely. By aligning resources with student needs and planning for long-term financial sustainability, we can preserve the excellence of PVPUSD for generations to come.

Future-Ready Learning

Technology should enhance learning, not replace meaningful instruction, creativity, collaboration, and personal connection.

Technology is changing how we learn, work, and connect. Our students should graduate with the digital literacy and critical thinking skills to thrive in an AI-driven world while also developing the uniquely human qualities that technology cannot replace.

Future-ready learning means fostering communication, collaboration, creativity, empathy, resilience, and ethical decision-making alongside strong academics. Technology should be used intentionally to enhance learning while preserving meaningful relationships, hands-on experiences, and exceptional teaching.

By preparing students academically, socially, and emotionally, we can ensure they are ready not only for the careers of tomorrow, but also to become thoughtful, engaged citizens and leaders.

A Place for Every Child

PVPUSD has earned its reputation for academic excellence by offering exceptional opportunities for students with a wide range of interests, talents, and goals. To remain one of California's premier school districts, we must continue evolving to meet the needs of today's families and tomorrow's learners.

Every student brings unique strengths, aspirations, and learning needs to the classroom. Whether a student is pursuing advanced academics, participating in the arts or athletics, exploring career pathways, or benefiting from additional academic or social-emotional support, every child should have the opportunity to learn, belong, and thrive in PVPUSD.

When families know their children will be challenged, supported, and valued, they choose public education. By continuing to invest in outstanding programs, exceptional educators, and opportunities that meet the diverse needs of our students, we can ensure PVPUSD remains a place where families want to enroll, stay, and succeed together.

Community Conversations

Let's Connect

Meeting members of the community, active listening, and maintaining an open dialogue are extremely important to me. Throughout my campaign, and continuing as a Governing Board Member, I am committed to staying connected with students, parents, educators, and community members.

Whether we're meeting over coffee, taking a neighborhood walk, or gathering with a small group, I would love the opportunity to hear your ideas, questions, and perspectives about our schools. Every voice matters, and I value conversations with members of our community who bring different experiences, viewpoints, and priorities to the table.

Schedule an Individual or Group Conversation with me:

  • 🏡 Neighborhood Gathering
